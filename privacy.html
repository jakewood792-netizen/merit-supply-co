Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect the information needed to fulfill your order and respond to you: name, email address, shipping address, and order details. Payment is processed entirely by PayPal — we never see or store your card or bank information.

How we use it

  • To process and ship your order
  • To respond to support requests
  • To send order/shipping updates (not marketing emails, unless you separately opt in)

Who we share it with

We share your order and shipping details only with the fulfillment partners needed to ship your specific order, and with PayPal to process payment. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies/analytics to understand traffic and improve the site. No personally identifying data is sold or shared through these tools.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@shopmeritsupplyco.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@shopmeritsupplyco.com.
