Shipping & Returns

Last updated: August 2026

Shipping times

Most orders ship directly from our fulfillment partners and typically arrive within 7-21 business days, depending on the item and your location. Some items may take longer during high-demand periods. You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Shipping costs

Free shipping on orders over $75. Orders under $75 have a flat shipping fee calculated at checkout.

Returns & refunds

Because items ship directly from our fulfillment partners rather than our own warehouse, we handle issues through refunds or replacements rather than mailed-in physical returns:

  • Wrong or damaged item: contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item — we'll send a replacement or a full refund.
  • Item never arrived: contact us if tracking shows no movement for 10+ business days past the estimated delivery window — we'll investigate and make it right.
  • Changed your mind: contact us within 14 days of delivery; refund eligibility depends on the item's condition and whether it's been used.

How to start a request

Email support@shopmeritsupplyco.com with your order number and a short description of the issue. See our Contact page for more.
